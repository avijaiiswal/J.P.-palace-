The Hotel

Central, comfortable, and close to everything Gonda

J.P Palace sits on the Faizabad road in front of the LIC office in Awas Vikas Colony — an easy walk from the railway station and bus stand. The hotel has welcomed travellers, wedding guests, and business visitors for years, with 18 well-kept rooms across three floors.

Every room comes with a flat-screen TV, wardrobe, hot & cold water, and round-the-clock room service. Downstairs, Zefran Restaurant serves multi-cuisine meals in a calm, music-lit dining room.
